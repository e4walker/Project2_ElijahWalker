This program is designed to show varying salary related stats using the data from the attached "salaries.csv" file. The stats generated by this program include the following:
- The average salary of entry-level jobs from 2024 compared with the average salary of entry-level jobs from 2023
- How much greater or less the median salary of entry-level jobs from 2024 is compared to the median of entry-level jobs from 2023
- The average salary of entry-level jobs from 2024 compared to the median salary of entry-level jobs from 2024
- The average salary of entry-level jobs from 2023 compared to the median salary of entry-level jobs from 2023
- The salary gain that would be observed by moving from an entry-level job to a mid-level job in 2024
- The median entry-level remote salary from 2024 compared with the median entry-level on-site salary from 2024
- The median all-level remote salary from 2024 compared with the median all-level on-site salary from 2024
- The top 5 highest paying entry-level jobs from 2024

To use the program, simply run it and all stats will be displayed with informational text describing what the values mean for each scenario.

All salary values are converted to int for simplicity.

Various colors are used throughout the program text, which each having a specific meaning:
- Blue: Informational header text
- Green: Salary value correlated to a positive result
- Red: Salary value correlated to a negative result
- Cyan: Salary value of good standing
- Yellow: Salary value of very good standing
